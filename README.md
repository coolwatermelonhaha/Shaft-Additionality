## 📜 Why I made this

I'm 11 years old. I like Minecraft, physics, and making games.

I made **Food Additionality** — a Minecraft food mod — in MCreator. Then I wanted to make **my own game** where the food system could be the main thing, not just an add-on.

This is that game. It's small. It's mine. And I'm making it one block at a time.
